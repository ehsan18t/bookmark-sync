Last updated {{PRIVACY_UPDATED}}

The short version
  • {{APP_NAME}} has no server. Your bookmarks go only to the storage you choose: your Google Drive or your own server.
  • We don't collect, track, sell or share anything. There are no analytics and no ads.
  • With Google Drive, the extension can only see the files it creates in a hidden app folder, never your other files.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the extension asks Google for one permission: drive.appdata, shown by Google as "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive". This gives it a hidden app folder in your Drive that only it can use.

  • What it accesses: only the files it created in that app folder. It can't see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • How it uses them: to store and read your bookmarks and their history, so your browsers stay in sync. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: the data isn't transferred to us or to anyone else, isn't used for advertising, and isn't read by people.
  • Removing access: remove {{APP_NAME}} in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections. To delete its files, open Google Drive, Settings, Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data for {{APP_NAME}}.

{{APP_NAME}}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your own server

If you use your own server (WebDAV, Nextcloud, rclone and others), your bookmarks are stored there and handled under that server's terms. If the server's address starts with http instead of https, your password and bookmarks travel unencrypted, and the extension warns you before connecting.

Encryption

Encryption is off unless you turn it on. Without it, whoever runs your storage (Google, or your server's operator) could read your bookmarks, as with any file you store there. With it, they only see scrambled data. The passphrase can't be recovered if you lose it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • The history of changes is kept for 7 days by default. You can change this, or turn it off, in Settings.
  • Records of deleted bookmarks stay in the sync files until the sync is deleted, so every browser can apply the deletion.
  • Delete this sync removes the bookmarks and history from your storage for every browser. A few small files naming the sync and its browsers stay, so the others learn it was deleted. You can delete the folder yourself.
  • Stop syncing here leaves your storage as it is.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything it kept in that browser.

Diagnostics

Export diagnostics in Settings creates a file on your computer with technical sync details, for reporting a problem. It contains no bookmarks, passwords or passphrases, and it goes nowhere unless you send it to someone.
